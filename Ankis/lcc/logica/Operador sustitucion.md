¿Cómo se define $\phi[\psi/p_{i}]$ 

---

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
    \perp[\phi/p_{i}]  & =\ \perp \\
    p_{j}[\phi/p_{i}]  & =\ 
        \begin{cases}
            \psi &  \text{ si }i = j \\
            p_{j} & \text{ si } i\neq j
        \end{cases} \\
    (\phi_{1}\square \phi_{2})[\phi/p_{i}] & =\ \phi_{1}[\phi/p_{i}] \ \square \ \phi_{2}[\phi/p_{i}]  \\
    (\neg \phi)[\phi/p_{i}]  & =\ \neg(\phi[\phi/p_{i}])
\end{array}
$$
