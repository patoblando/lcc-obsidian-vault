Sean $t \in \mathrm{TERM}$ y $\phi \in \mathrm{Form}$, definimos $\phi[t/x_{i}]$ por recursión en $\phi$:

$$
\begin{array}{lcl}
\perp [t/x_{i}] & = & \perp \\
P_{i}(t_{1},\dots,t_{n})[t/x_{i}] & = & P_{i}(t_{1}[t/x_{1}],\dots,t_{n}[t/x_{1}]) \\
(\neg \phi)[t/x_{1}]  & = & \neg(\phi [t/x_{1}]) \\

\end{array}
$$
